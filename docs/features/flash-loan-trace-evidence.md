# Flash-loan trace evidence and limits

Provider rules cover Aave flash-loan events/callbacks, Balancer Vault flash loans, Uniswap flash swaps/callbacks, and Maker flash mint callbacks. Trace availability depends on RPC support and retention. Log-only reconstruction cannot prove internal call order. The maximum range is 5,000 blocks and 250 transactions; timeouts and coverage notices prevent an incomplete scan from appearing complete.
